# Hybrid and Work From Home Policy

## Hybrid schedule
Office-based employees work from office 3 days a week. Teams decide their anchor days with their manager. Employees hired as Remote are exempt from the office requirement.

## Work from home beyond the hybrid schedule
Up to 10 extra work-from-home days per quarter can be taken with manager approval, for example for travel or caregiving. These do not need a reason to be shared with HR.

## Work from another city
Working from another city in India is allowed for up to 30 days a year with manager approval. Working from outside India is not permitted due to data-security and tax rules, except with written approval from the CISO and HR.

## Home office allowance
Every employee gets a one-time home office allowance of Rs 15,000 for a chair, monitor or desk, claimable through reimbursements within their first 6 months.

## Internet allowance
Employees receive an internet allowance of Rs 1,500 per month, paid with salary. No bills are required.
