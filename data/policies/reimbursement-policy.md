# Travel and Reimbursement Policy

## Submitting claims
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense with original bills. Claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle after manager approval. Claims older than 90 days are not processed.

## Domestic travel
Economy air travel is allowed for journeys longer than 6 hours by train. Hotel limits are Rs 6,000 per night in metro cities and Rs 4,000 in other cities. Daily food allowance is Rs 1,200 for L1-L2 and Rs 1,800 for L3-L4.

## Local conveyance
Cab rides for client meetings are reimbursed at actuals. Late-night cabs after 9 PM from office to home are reimbursed for all employees, with priority on women's safety; use the company cab partner where available.

## Learning and certification budget
Every employee has a yearly learning budget of Rs 40,000 for courses, certifications and conferences. Spends above Rs 15,000 in one go need manager approval. If an employee leaves within 6 months of a certification paid by the company, the cost is recovered pro-rata.

## Mobile phone
Managers (L4) are reimbursed up to Rs 1,000 per month for mobile bills.
