# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) and Grievance Policy

## Zero tolerance
The company has zero tolerance for sexual harassment, discrimination or bullying. This policy follows the POSH Act, 2013.

## Internal Committee
The Internal Committee (IC) is headed by a senior woman employee and includes an external member. Complaints can be raised by email to ic@peopleos.demo or through a confidential ticket. Complaints should be raised within 3 months of the incident; the IC can extend this by another 3 months.

## Confidentiality and no retaliation
The identity of the complainant, respondent and witnesses is kept confidential. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a serious misconduct.

## Other grievances
Concerns about managers, pay fairness or unethical behaviour can be raised with your HR Business Partner or anonymously through the ethics hotline at ethics@peopleos.demo. Every grievance gets a first response within 2 working days.

## Employee Assistance Programme
Free and confidential counselling is available 24x7 for employees and their families through the Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) at 1800-000-0000. HR never sees who used the service.
