Set up in three steps

  1. Create your workspaceSign up as the HR admin. Upload your leave, WFH, reimbursement and other policies as PDF or Word files.
  2. Add your peopleImport a CSV with names, emails, titles and managers. Share each person's invite link on email or WhatsApp.
  3. Your team starts askingEmployees get answers with sources and request leave. Managers approve from their own screen. HR sees everything in one console.

What your team can do

Ask about any policy

Answers come only from your own documents, with the section they came from. If the policy doesn't cover it, PeopleOS says so and offers to ask HR.

Leave and helpdesk

Check balances, apply or cancel leave, raise IT, payroll or facilities tickets. Nothing is submitted until the person confirms.

People data for HR

"Attrition by department", "who has the most leave this year": answers as charts, with the query shown so you can check it.

Career guidance

Shows which parts of a role AI can take over and which skills to grow, with the scoring explained.

Built for HR data

  • Your data stays yours. Each company's policies and people are kept separate. The AI only ever sees your company's records.
  • Role-based access. Employees see their own records. Managers see their team. Only HR sees salaries, ratings and personal details.
  • A person stays in charge. The assistant never submits anything without a click, and leave still needs a manager's approval.
  • Sensitive topics go to people. Harassment, mental health or grievances are routed to your HR contacts, never answered by AI.
