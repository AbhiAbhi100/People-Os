# Benefits and Insurance Policy

## Group health insurance
Employees are covered by group medical insurance of Rs 5 lakh per year, covering employee, spouse and up to two children. Parents or parents-in-law can be added at a subsidised premium deducted from salary. Pre-existing conditions are covered from day one.

## Adding dependents
New dependents (spouse after marriage, newborn) must be added within 30 days of the event by raising a Benefits ticket with proof. Outside this window, dependents can only be added at the annual renewal in April.

## Term life and accident cover
Every employee has term life cover of 3 times annual CTC and personal accident cover of Rs 25 lakh.

## Wellness
Annual health check-ups are free for employees above 30. A wellness reimbursement of Rs 6,000 per year covers gym, yoga or sports memberships.

## Employee stock options
Eligible employees at L3 and above may receive ESOPs with a 4-year vesting schedule and a 1-year cliff. Grant details are shared in the offer letter or during the annual cycle.
