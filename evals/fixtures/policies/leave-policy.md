# Leave Policy

## Leave types and annual entitlement
Every full-time employee gets 18 Earned Leaves (EL), 8 Sick Leaves (SL) and 6 Casual Leaves (CL) per calendar year. Entitlement is credited on 1 January and pro-rated for joiners in their first year.

## Applying for leave
Leave is applied through the PeopleOS assistant or the HRMS portal. Earned Leave must be applied at least 3 working days in advance. Sick Leave can be applied on the same day or retroactively within 2 working days. Leave of more than 5 consecutive working days needs manager approval in advance.

## Sick leave and medical certificate
A medical certificate is required for Sick Leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Upload it within 7 days of returning to work.

## Carry forward and encashment
Up to 30 unused Earned Leaves can be carried forward to the next year. Earned Leave above 30 lapses on 31 December. Earned Leave is encashed at basic salary only at the time of exit. Sick and Casual Leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## Weekends and holidays
Weekends and public holidays falling inside a leave period are not counted as leave days. The company follows 12 public holidays a year; the list is published in January.

## Parental leave
Birthing parents get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Non-birthing parents get 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, usable within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption. Adoptive parents of a child under 3 months get 12 weeks.

## Bereavement leave
Employees get 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member.
